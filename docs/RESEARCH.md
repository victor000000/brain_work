# Research workflow

## Scope, policy and implemented checks

Numeric signals use verified PV/options measurements; GROUP metadata may partition observations. Archive ideas from other categories without treating archival as simulation authorization. Provider category/MATRIX type/name prefix is insufficient proof of meaning. Ambiguous fields require native descriptions, provenance, timing and coverage review.

The retained November 7 design specifies atom alphas: exactly one distinct numeric measurement field, including dependencies hidden in variables. Repeated uses of that measurement are allowed. This is a research admission policy, **not a structural guarantee of published 1.0.1**. Do not infer atom compliance from a one-field-looking string.

`core/scope.py` screens identifier names, excludes known operators and classifies numeric leaves. Semantic EPS/EBIT/EBT/assets tokens override PV/options prefixes; unresolved or prohibited leaves return review/violation. `runner/submit/flow.py` applies this verdict to available regular-expression payloads before new submission work. Already-owned requests reconcile first. The collector can abstain on unreadable/missing payloads; it is not a comprehensive fail-closed parser. It also lacks full partition-role/variable analysis. Metadata validation and manual admission remain necessary.

`runner/probe_validation.py` checks cell/settings, native operators, syntax, field/type pools and the 64-operation FASTEXPR limit before probe dispatch. Python probes use their own source checks. Neither local syntax success nor category filtering proves native qualification.

## Finite experiment contract

1. Read the source idea; save author/date/source and distinguish its claim from measured evidence.
2. State one economic question, expected direction, component control and stop rule.
3. Verify exact field/cell, numeric meaning, type/units, maturity, publication timing and missing/stale-value semantics. GROUP codes are not numeric measurements.
4. Deduplicate exact and equivalent prior trials and protected uncertain payloads. Declare history gaps.
5. Freeze expression/settings/source hashes and one immutable owner before dispatch. Never tune the control after seeing results.
6. Submit only through the authorized owner. Record native parent/child/alpha handles and verify expression/settings identity.
7. Assess every case, including errors, recent-period/robust-universe failures and unresolved checks, before another finite study.

No arbitrary field replacement, epsilon padding, known-FAIL submission or unbounded settings grid. Failure of a sampled mechanism does not prove all PV/options signals impossible. Historical TEST repeatedly viewed during tuning is reused validation, not untouched OS evidence.

## Evidence stages

A native simulation201 receipt is not a completed alpha; COMPLETE is not quality qualification. Full native relevant checks and correlation verdicts are needed. A submission201 acknowledgement is not verified acceptance: verify native dateSubmitted and ACTIVE for accepted-inventory claims. Verify quarterly attribution separately. Published combined OS scores and actual Expert promotion are separate evidence again.

Cell-specific native limits override generic historical thresholds. Missing/PENDING/ERROR checks are unresolved. Use the check/ownership implementation and current account state, not a historical report, to decide the next action.

## Expert planning and portfolio evidence

The [November 7 design](EXPERT_PV_OPTIONS_DESIGN_20261107.md) records the retained route: audit truly untested single-field mechanisms, release bounded studies only when justified, complete five additional pyramids and improve actual published combined performance. Its account figures and historical 20-signal/10-pyramid/0.50 targets require native refresh; this guide does not certify current requirements or promotion timing.

Record all eligible published combined measures independently; null stays unavailable. Per-alpha Sharpe averages do not reconstruct portfolio performance. Require aligned native PnL/date semantics before contribution or cost attribution. Acceptance alone cannot establish profit, live trading readiness or a causal explanation for score changes.
